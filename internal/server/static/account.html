Your account

Connect an app

Show a QR code and scan it in the app, or give a desktop app the link below it. It works once and expires after fifteen minutes, and the app then gets its own key.

A desktop app with no camera can take the same pairing as a link. Anyone who has it can pair a device for fifteen minutes, so treat it like the QR code.

Approve a device

Some apps show a short code and ask you to type it here, instead of a QR code. Type the code exactly as the app shows it. It works once and expires after ten minutes.

Ending the app's access signs it out at its next refresh. It does not touch the devices paired with a QR code.

Devices

Apps paired with this account. Each one signs its requests with a key that stays on the device. Remove a device to disconnect it at its next request.

Waiting for confirmation

The phone that scanned the QR code shows a six-digit code. Choose Code matches only if it is the same code as here, on a phone you are holding now, and confirm on the phone too. A code that differs means someone else scanned the QR code: reject it. A pairing that is not confirmed on both sides expires with its QR code.
